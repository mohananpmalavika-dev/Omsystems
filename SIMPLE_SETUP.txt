========================================
  SENTINEL CAMERA SYSTEM
  Simple Setup Guide for Everyone
========================================

No technical knowledge needed! Just follow these 3 easy steps:


STEP 1: Install the System
---------------------------

1. Find the file: INSTALL_SENTINEL.bat
2. Right-click on it
3. Select "Run as Administrator"
4. Wait 2-3 minutes while it installs
5. Your web browser will open automatically

✓ Done! The system is now running in the background.


STEP 2: View Your Dashboard
----------------------------

Your dashboard opens automatically, or you can click the
"Sentinel Dashboard" icon on your desktop anytime.

Dashboard Link: https://sentinel-grid-monitoring1.onrender.com

What you'll see:
• Your location (branch)
• System health status
• Camera list (will appear after Step 3)


STEP 3: Add Cameras
--------------------

The system will automatically find cameras on your network!

To add cameras:

1. In your dashboard, click on your branch/location name
2. Scroll down to see "My Cameras" section
3. Cameras will appear here automatically

If cameras show "Need Login":
• Look for the blue section that says "Having trouble with camera passwords?"
• Click "Update Camera Credentials" button
• Try common passwords or let it test automatically
• Click "Apply Credentials"

✓ Done! Cameras should now start working.


========================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
========================================

Camera not showing?
------------------
• Make sure cameras are connected to the same network
• Wait 2-3 minutes for auto-discovery
• Check if cameras are powered on

Camera says "Need Login"?
------------------------
• Click "Update Camera Credentials" button
• Try these common passwords:
  - Empty (no password)
  - admin
  - 12345
  - 123456
  - 888888
• Or use the password from your camera manual

System not starting?
-------------------
1. Open Task Scheduler (Windows search for "Task Scheduler")
2. Look for "Sentinel Camera System"
3. Right-click and select "Run"


========================================
NEED HELP?
========================================

Contact your system administrator or IT support team.

Dashboard: https://sentinel-grid-monitoring1.onrender.com


========================================
UNINSTALLING
========================================

If you need to remove the system:

1. Go to: C:\Users\[YourName]\Sentinel Camera System
2. Run: Uninstall.bat
