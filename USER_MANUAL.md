# KryptonVision User Manual

## 1. Overview

KryptonVision is a live surveillance and security operations platform for monitoring cameras, reviewing alerts, and managing live video wall operations across branches and zones.

This manual covers the most important workflows for operators, supervisors, and administrators.

---

## 2. Logging In

1. Open the application in your browser.
2. Enter your username and password.
3. If your account has multiple roles, select the correct access profile.
4. After login, you will land on the dashboard or command center.

If login fails:
- confirm your password is correct
- confirm your account is active
- check whether your session expired and log in again

---

## 3. Main Navigation

From the left navigation panel, you can access:

- Overview
- Live Video Wall
- Alerts
- Branches
- Analytics
- Maintenance
- Organization
- Security operations

Use the navigation menu to switch between monitoring, alert review, system health, and administration.

---

## 4. Live Video Wall

The Live Video Wall shows real-time CCTV feeds from configured cameras.

### Accessing the Live Wall

1. Click Live or Live Video Wall from the left navigation.
2. Select the zone or region if required.
3. Review the video tiles shown on the wall.

### Understanding the Tile States

- LIVE: the feed is active and streaming.
- CONNECTING: the stream is being established.
- RECONNECTING: the stream dropped and is retrying.
- ERROR: the feed failed to connect.
- OFFLINE: the camera is unavailable.

### Refreshing the Wall

- Use the Refresh button in the top-right area of the Live Wall to reload current system state.

### Camera Actions

Depending on the tile, you may be able to:
- open the camera stream in full view
- view AI overlays
- review alerts tied to that camera
- start or retry a live feed

---

## 5. Alerts

The alerts module is used to monitor events such as:
- no helmet detection
- unauthorized access
- motion or suspicious activity
- system health warnings

### Reviewing Alerts

1. Open Alerts.
2. Check the newest events first.
3. Click an alert to view camera details, timestamp, and related context.
4. Use the camera feed to confirm whether the alert is valid.

### Responding to Alerts

- validate the camera feed
- escalate when needed
- record action taken if required by your SOP

---

## 6. Camera Monitoring

### Viewing a Camera Live

1. Click the camera tile or open the camera detail view.
2. Wait for the connection to complete.
3. If the feed is delayed, click Retry if available.

### If the Camera Shows White or Connecting

1. Wait a few seconds for the stream to reconnect automatically.
2. Refresh the camera tile or the Live Wall.
3. Ensure the camera is online and reachable.
4. If the issue continues, contact the technical support or admin team.

---

## 7. AI and Detection Features

The platform can run AI-based monitoring such as:
- helmet detection
- person recognition
- event classification
- rule-based alerts

These features appear as overlays or automated alerts tied to camera activity. Not all cameras may have AI enabled depending on deployment and permissions.

---

## 8. Branch and Zone Monitoring

If your environment uses multiple branches or zones:

- select a specific zone to narrow the wall view
- filter by region or branch as needed
- use priority camera or alert views to focus on critical areas

---

## 9. Maintenance and Health

Use the Maintenance or Health section to check:
- platform status
- infrastructure health
- camera availability
- service reliability

This is useful when the system appears slow, a camera is not streaming, or an alert is not generating as expected.

---

## 10. Common Troubleshooting

### Camera is offline

- confirm the upstream device is powered on
- check network connectivity
- verify camera configuration
- refresh the wall

### White screen / connecting state persists

- wait for automatic recovery
- retry the stream
- refresh the page
- check whether the camera is reporting healthy status

### Alerts are missing

- confirm the rule is enabled
- ensure the camera is active and streaming
- verify the AI feature is available for that camera
- review the alert list for suppression or rule configuration issues

---

## 11. Best Practices

- keep the Live Wall focused on the most relevant zones
- review alerts promptly
- reload the wall if a camera is stalled
- avoid leaving disconnected cameras in a critical monitoring section
- use health pages to confirm service status before escalation

---

## 12. Support and Escalation

If a problem continues after refresh or retry:

1. Check system health and maintenance pages.
2. Confirm the camera is connected to the network.
3. Note the camera name, time of issue, and tile state.
4. Escalate to the technical/admin team with this information.

---

## 13. Quick Reference

- Live Wall: monitoring and real-time camera viewing
- Alerts: incidents, detections, and events
- Health: system and infrastructure status
- Branches: site or region-level view
- Analytics: reporting and intelligence

---

## 14. Contact and Access Notes

Use your assigned operator or administrator role for the correct permissions. If you do not see a camera, alert, or management function, contact the system administrator.

This manual is for general operational use and may be updated as new features are added.
