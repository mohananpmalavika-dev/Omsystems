# End-User Manual

## Scope

This guide is for operators and investigators using the dashboard. Menu
visibility and available actions depend on assigned permissions, enabled
features, service configuration, and the deployment's device inventory.

## Open the dashboard

1. Navigate to the URL provided by your administrator.
2. Sign in using the identity method configured for your organization.
3. Confirm the organization/branch context shown by the application.
4. Use only authorized branches and devices. If expected data is absent, ask an
   administrator to check scope, data freshness, and feature configuration.

## View cameras and video

Use the live/video or operations camera pages to find an authorized camera.
Select a camera and request live view when available. Video requires the camera
to be reachable, correctly configured, supported by the media path, and
authorized for your account. A blank player can indicate a stream, network,
browser transport, credential, or permission issue; it does not prove that the
camera is physically offline.

The dashboard also contains playback, bookmarks, video search, and synchronized
playback pages. Results depend on indexed recordings, retention, permissions,
and enabled services.

## Review alerts and incidents

Use alert/command-center and incident pages to review events. Where permitted,
the interface may provide acknowledge, assign, escalate, comment, or
investigation actions. Follow your organization's response policy and verify
that external notifications were delivered when required.

## Preserve evidence

Use the evidence and investigation workflows for approved capture, review, and
export. Follow local chain-of-custody, redaction, retention, and legal-hold
procedures. Do not download or redistribute footage unless you are authorized.

## Understand status

Health pages can report stale, unavailable, estimated, or unknown readings.
Check the observation time and reason/context before interpreting a status.
Maintenance or telemetry pages may require an administrator to correct
inventory or connectivity.

## Get help safely

Provide support staff with the time range, affected branch/camera identifier,
screen/page, and a redacted error message. Do not send passwords, session
cookies, tokens, camera credentials, private recordings, or personal data
through unapproved channels.
