# Banking surveillance positioning

## The promise

**Protect branch uptime. Investigate risk fast. Preserve verifiable evidence.**

Sentinel Grid is a banking surveillance operations platform for organisations that need to keep branch video coverage dependable, identify ATM, vault, and cash-area risk early, resolve events quickly, and retain evidence with verifiable integrity and custody history.

## What the product leads with

1. **Branch uptime** — camera, recorder, network, storage, and recording-continuity health are operational outcomes, not background metrics.
2. **Banking risk** — prioritise ATM, vault, locker, cash-area, branch perimeter, and cash-logistics workflows over generic AI feature lists.
3. **Fast investigation** — move from an alert to scoped video search, synchronized playback, incident ownership, and response without losing branch context.
4. **Verifiable evidence** — preserve source-media hashes, signed manifests, legal holds, redaction records, and chain-of-custody events.

## Customer-facing language

Use:

- “Banking surveillance operations”
- “Protect branch uptime”
- “ATM, vault, and cash-area risk”
- “Investigate fast”
- “Evidence integrity and custody”
- “Verifiable evidence trail”

Avoid:

- “100 AI features” as a primary value statement
- “Most trusted” until independent security assessment, field certification, reference customers, and legal acceptance evidence support that claim
- “Court-admissible” as an unconditional claim; use “verifiable integrity and custody” unless the relevant jurisdiction’s certified workflow has been completed
- Any live, healthy, compliant, or certified status unless confirmed by the relevant data source and acceptance record

## Product flow

`Protect coverage → Find video → Review footage → Resolve risk → Preserve proof`

| Outcome | Primary product surfaces |
| --- | --- |
| Protect branch uptime | Command Center, Live Branch Coverage, Camera & Device Uptime, Recording Continuity, Maintenance Work Orders |
| Surface banking risk | ATM/Vault/Cash-Area Risk, security alerts, branch risk intelligence, watchlists, cash logistics |
| Investigate fast | Video search, synchronized playback, recording continuity, incidents |
| Preserve proof | Evidence integrity & custody, legal holds, signed exports, compliance evidence |

## Release claim gate

The promise is a product direction. Stronger external claims require proof:

1. Physical camera/DVR/NVR certification by exact model and firmware.
2. Camera-to-recording-to-playback-to-signed-evidence acceptance artifacts.
3. Real Redis/PostgreSQL/media-node failover results with measured RTO/RPO.
4. Tenant and branch isolation penetration testing for every media and evidence endpoint.
5. Counsel-approved jurisdiction-specific electronic-record certificate workflow.
6. Independent VAPT and signed production release evidence.
