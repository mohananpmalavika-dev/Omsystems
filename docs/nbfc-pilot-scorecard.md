## Pilot cadence

1. Week 0: select one region/branch cohort, baseline outcomes, validate integrations and escalation contacts.
2. Week 1: conduct controlled onboarding and live-view/evidence recovery drill.
3. Weeks 2–5: weekly outcome review; resolve reliability and workflow defects.
4. Week 6: conduct an incident and audit-readiness drill; collect sponsor feedback.
5. Week 8: agree results, renewal/expansion decision, and any publishable case-study language.

The product must show unavailable data as unavailable. Do not use pilot scorecards to infer, estimate, or manufacture operational results.
